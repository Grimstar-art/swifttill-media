SwiftTill Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026. This policy covers the SwiftTill POS software and SwiftTill Technologies' own social media, WhatsApp, TikTok and e-mail accounts.

1. Shop data in SwiftTill POS

A shop's sales, stock, customer and staff records belong to the shop. They are kept on the shop's own computers. If the shop turns on sync between tills, cloud backup or other online features, copies are sent to the SwiftTill server so those features can work. SwiftTill does not sell or rent shop data and does not use it for advertising.

For licensing, updates and support, the software sends a limited amount of technical information: a device identifier, the computer's name, the software version, licence and package details, the shop name and contact details given at activation, and trimmed error reports that do not include sales or customer lists.

For the personal data of a shop's customers and staff, the shop is the data controller under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Where SwiftTill stores it for the shop (sync or cloud backup), SwiftTill acts as a data processor, only on the shop's instructions, and keeps it confidential.

2. Messages to SwiftTill

When you message, comment on, or e-mail SwiftTill (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok or e-mail), we receive your public profile name, your message and the time it was sent. We use them only to answer you and to follow up on your request. Some replies are prepared by automated tools working on our behalf; questions about money, complaints and licences are passed to a person.

We do not sell this information, we do not add you to marketing lists without asking, and we do not start conversations with people who have not contacted us.

3. Content we publish on TikTok and other platforms

SwiftTill uses each platform's official tools to publish its own posts and videos to its own accounts. We do not access other users' private data through these tools.

4. How long we keep it

Messages are kept for as long as needed to help you, and in any case no longer than the law allows. Licence and payment records are kept as tax law requires.

5. Your rights

You may ask what personal information SwiftTill holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, unless the law requires us to keep it. Contact us at swifttilltechnologies@gmail.com or 0743898282.
